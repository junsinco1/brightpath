BrightPath Kids — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

BrightPath Kids is a local-first app. We take children's privacy seriously, and our architecture makes it simple to describe:

What we collect

Nothing. BrightPath Kids collects no personal data, no names, no location, no contacts, and no usage analytics. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no tracking technologies of any kind.

Where your data lives

All family settings, goals, lessons, streaks, and pretend-money records are stored only on the device the app runs on. They never leave the device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Network & encryption

The app makes no network connections and communicates with no servers. It contains no encryption beyond what the operating system itself provides for local storage.

Ads & purchases

The app shows no third-party advertising and no third-party content. All money in the app is pretend money used for learning; real payments are not processed by the app.

Children's privacy

Because BrightPath Kids collects no data whatsoever, there is nothing to collect, store, share, or delete. Families can remove all app data at any time by deleting the app from the device.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/junsinco1/BrightPathKids.
