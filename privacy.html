BrightPath Money for Kids — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

BrightPath Money for Kids is an offline financial learning app for families. All balances and transactions are for practice only. The app does not connect to banks or process real payments.

What the app saves on your device

The app saves a learner’s nickname, selected learning mode, practice balances and transactions, savings goals, allowance and chore settings and progress, and lesson and activity progress. It uses these records to keep each learner’s practice and settings available between sessions. Use a nickname rather than a full name.

What the app collects or shares

There are no online accounts, ads, third-party analytics, tracking, or cloud sync. The app does not send learner records or device identifiers to us or to third parties. It does not ask for an email address, birth date, bank details, or location.

How to delete a learner profile

From Profiles & caregiver, select the learner, open Profiles & caregiver again if needed, then choose Delete active profile and confirm. This removes the selected profile and its associated practice records, settings, and progress from local storage. Records remain on the device until removed; use this in-app control rather than relying on uninstalling.

External websites

If a parent or caregiver opens the public privacy policy or support tracker in a browser, the website’s own privacy practices apply. The app does not attach learner records or profile identifiers to the link.

BrightPath Money for Kids support

Parents and caregivers: visit our support tracker to ask a question or report a problem. Select New issue and sign in to GitHub to post.

Support requests are public. Please describe the problem without sharing names, contact details, screenshots of family profiles, or other personal information.
